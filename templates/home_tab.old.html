About Us

M/S. Shreeji SteelTubes Pvt. Ltd. (ISO 9001:2015 Certified) is one of India’s leading distributors, stockists, and suppliers of Iron & Steel products, known nationwide as the “House of Quality Steel Tubes™.”

With over five decades of legacy and a state-of-the-art 80,000 sq. ft. warehouse at Kalamboli — the heart of Maharashtra’s steel hub — we offer an unmatched range of GI, MS, GP, ERW, Seamless, and Structural Hollow Section pipes (CHS / SHS / RHS) in multiple grades and dimensions.

We are proud to be authorized distributors of India’s most trusted brands including Jindal Star, Tata Structura, Hi-Tech, Maharashtra Seamless Ltd., Jindal Panther (JSPL), Asian, Narmada, Rama, Khanna, JTL, SRJ and more, ensuring that every product delivered reflects authenticity, reliability, and excellence.

Driven by our core values of Integrity, Quality, and Commitment, we continue to build strong and long-lasting relationships with over 1,500+ satisfied clients across Infrastructure, Engineering, Oil & Gas, Power, and Fabrication industries throughout India.
